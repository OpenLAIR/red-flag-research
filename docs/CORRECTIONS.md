# Corrections

**Think a result is wrong?** If you are an author and believe a result about
your paper is wrong, email **red.flag.autoresearch@gmail.com**. We will correct it right
away, and we take the result off the live page and the public data while we
check.

Please use e-mail rather than a public issue: it is private, needs no GitHub
account, and does not draw public attention to your paper.

## What a result means

A **Source review recommended** label means the system found passages,
numbers or tables that closely match an earlier public paper without a clear
attribution, so a person should compare the two papers side by side. It is
**not** a finding of plagiarism, misconduct or bad faith, and it has not been
reviewed by the conference, the authors or any integrity committee. Shared
authors, an earlier version of the same work, a cited dataset or a common
baseline can all explain matching text, and the system may not see them.

## What we do when we receive a report

1. **Take the result down.** As soon as a maintainer reads the report, the
   result is removed from the live page and marked as under review in the
   public data. We do not wait for the check to finish.
2. **Acknowledge.** We reply to you, normally within one working day.
3. **Check.** A maintainer compares the two papers using your explanation and
   the saved evidence.
4. **Correct.** If the result is wrong, we correct it in the live page and the
   public data, note the correction in the changelog without naming the paper,
   and tell you. If the check finds the result was right, we tell you why before
   anything is shown again.

We do not publish your report or your contact details.

## Benchmark cases

Benchmark cases are anonymized. If you think a benchmark label is wrong, refer
to the case by its case id only.
